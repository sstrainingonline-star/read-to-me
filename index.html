QUICK GUIDE

How to use Read To Me

  1. Tap Start live reader and allow camera access.
  2. Hold the phone steady over the printed question.
  3. When the words are recognised, tap Read aloud — or turn on Auto-read.
  4. Move to the next question. The reader will detect new, steady text.

You can also upload or take a photo instead of using live camera mode. Good lighting, a flat page and steady focus improve recognition.
